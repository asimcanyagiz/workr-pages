Connect your Claude

Let your own Anthropic Claude read and edit your Workr tasks — straight from Claude on your computer. Ask Claude to add a task, tick one off, tidy up a project, and it shows up in Workr.

Opt-in, off by default, your data stays yours. Nothing is shared until you switch this on inside the Workr app and tap through a consent screen. You can disconnect at any time, and your tasks on your device and in iCloud are never touched.

How to set it up

Two steps — one in the Workr app, one in Claude. You sign in with the same Apple ID on both sides so they connect to each other.

  1. In the Workr app: open Settings → Connect your Claude, read the consent screen, and sign in. This puts a private mirror of your tasks in the cloud so Claude has something to read and write. (It is created only when you do this, and removed when you disconnect.)
  2. In Claude (claude.ai or the Claude app, on your computer): go to Settings → Connectors → "Add custom connector", paste the connector link below, and sign in with the same Apple ID you used in the Workr app.

What you can ask Claude to do

Once connected, just talk to Claude in plain language. Here is everything it can do today, with an example of what to say:

What it doesExample prompt
List tasks
See your tasks — open (default), done, or all, and optionally just one project.		"List my open Workr tasks"
List projects
See the projects you've created.		"What projects do I have in Workr?"
Add a task
Create a task, optionally with a priority (low / medium / high) and assigned to an existing project.		"Add 'Buy milk' to Workr, high priority"
Complete / reopen a task
Tick a task off, or bring it back to your open list.		"Mark 'Buy milk' as done in Workr"
Update a task
Change a task's title, priority, project, or pin it.		"Rename my 'Buy milk' task to 'Buy oat milk'"
Delete a task
Remove a task. It's a soft delete — recoverable on your device.		"Delete the 'Buy milk' task in Workr"
Add a project
Create a new project to organise tasks into.		"Create a 'Groceries' project in Workr"
Changes apply on your next device sync. When you ask Claude to add, change, or remove something, Claude queues the change — the Workr app applies it the next time it syncs, so it isn't instant. Reading your tasks is always live.

This list of commands grows as Workr grows — new things you can ask Claude to do will appear here.

Privacy & control

  • Off by default. The feature does nothing until you explicitly turn it on in the Workr app.
  • Explicit consent. Before anything is shared, a consent screen names exactly who is involved: Anthropic (your Claude, the recipient), Google Firebase (stores your private task mirror), and WorkOS (handles the sign-in).
  • Only your tasks, never your photos. The mirror contains task titles, notes, projects, priority, and completion state. Photos attached to tasks are never shared.
  • Disconnect stops sharing and deletes the cloud copy.
  • Delete Connected Account removes the mirror and your sign-in identity entirely.
  • Your real data is never affected. Your tasks on your device and in your private iCloud stay exactly as they are, whatever you do here.

For the full detail on the third parties involved and what is shared, see Privacy Policy §7 (Third-party services).
